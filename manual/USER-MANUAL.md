# Neighbor User Manual

Neighbor is agent software for the people. It helps you write, organize, and check work using the documents you already have. It runs on a regular computer. You do not need a special graphics card.

This manual is written for people who know little or nothing about AI, including older adults and anyone who finds technology hard to navigate.

---

## 1. What Neighbor does

You give it your own notes, letters, spreadsheets, or emails. You ask it to draft something or answer a question. It writes an answer. Then it checks every factual statement in that answer against the documents you provided.

- **Supported** means the fact appears in your documents.
- **Unsupported** means it does not. Neighbor will not let you copy or share until you fix or remove that statement.

Your private information stays on this computer unless you explicitly choose a cloud model and confirm a clear warning.

## 2. Who it is for

- A musician writing a press bio or a set list from their own notes
- An artist describing a show from their own records
- A small business owner summarizing invoices or drafting a customer email
- A teacher preparing a parent note or a lesson summary
- A nonprofit writing a grant from their program documents
- A scientist drafting a methods paragraph from lab notes
- An older adult writing a letter to a doctor, a bank, or a family member
- Anyone who wants an agent that stays on their computer and speaks plain language

## 3. Getting started

1. Open Neighbor.
2. The program starts its helper in the background if it is not already running. You will see a status light in the header.
3. Choose one of three simple paths:
   - **Search my files** — pick folders that contain your notes or documents
   - **Connect email** — optional; messages stay on this computer by default
   - **Start** — open the chat and begin

You do not need to change any technical settings.

## 4. Starting, stopping, and resetting

The header shows a colored dot and three buttons:

- **Start** — turns the helper on if it is stopped
- **Stop** — turns the helper off
- **Reset** — stops and starts it again (useful if something seems stuck)

The program tries to start the helper automatically when you open it.

## 5. Chatting

Type a question or a request in the box at the bottom and press Send. The answer appears above. Below the answer you will see a short list of claims.

Example: you type “Write a short bio from my notes.” The answer appears. Under it:

- Supported: “Released three albums since 2018” (found in your notes)
- Unsupported: “Sold out Madison Square Garden” (not in your notes)

Use the Fix button on an unsupported claim, or edit the answer yourself. Copy and Share stay gray until every claim is supported or removed.

## 6. The care slider

A slider above the chat sets how carefully Neighbor checks its own work:

- 1 — Fast
- 2 — Standard (recommended)
- 3 — Strict

It never turns checking off. It only changes how many times the agent looks.

## 7. Detailed examples

### Musician — press bio

You have a notes file that says: “Three albums since 2018. Played the Bluebird Cafe in 2023. Based in Nashville.”

You ask: “Write a one-paragraph bio.”

Neighbor drafts a bio. It marks “Three albums since 2018” and “Played the Bluebird Cafe” as supported. If it adds “Grammy-nominated,” that claim is marked unsupported. You cannot copy the bio until you remove or correct that line.

### Artist — show description

Your records say: “Solo show at the community gallery, March 2024. Twenty paintings. Opening night had 80 visitors.”

You ask: “Write a short description of the show for my website.”

Supported claims stay. Any invented detail (a museum name, a sale price you never wrote down) is flagged.

### Small business — customer email

You paste last month’s invoice totals. You ask: “Draft a polite email reminding the customer of the balance.”

The balance figure is supported if it matches your numbers. A made-up due date is unsupported.

### Teacher — parent note

You have notes: “Maya completed the reading log. She missed two homework assignments.”

You ask: “Write a short note to Maya’s parent.”

The note can mention the reading log and the two missed assignments. It cannot invent a grade or a behavior incident that is not in your notes.

### Nonprofit — grant paragraph

Your program document says: “Served 120 families in 2025. Offered free tax help on Saturdays.”

You ask: “Write one paragraph for the grant about our impact.”

“120 families” and “free tax help on Saturdays” are supported. A claim like “reduced poverty by 40%” is unsupported unless that number is in your document.

### Scientist — methods paragraph

Your lab notes say: “Samples were stored at 4 °C. Assay run in triplicate.”

You ask: “Draft the methods sentence.”

The temperature and the triplicate run are supported. An invented instrument model is flagged.

### Older adult — letter to a doctor

You type: “I take 10 mg of lisinopril in the morning. My blood pressure at the last visit was 138/82.”

You ask: “Write a short letter to my doctor about my blood pressure.”

Those two facts are supported. The program will not add a diagnosis or a new medication that you did not write.

## 8. Files

Click Add folder and choose a folder of notes, PDFs, or spreadsheets. Only the folders you add are searched. Supported formats include PDF, CSV, and plain text.

## 9. Email

Connecting email is optional. When you use the local model, message bodies stay on this computer. If you later switch to a cloud model, you will see a clear warning before anything is sent.

## 10. Charts

You can ask for a simple chart of numbers you already have (for example, monthly sales or attendance). Choose bar, line, or scatter. You can export a picture.

## 11. Privacy

- The default model runs on this device.
- Your documents are not sent anywhere unless you choose a cloud model and confirm the warning.
- Every action that would send or change something shows a preview first.
- Content taken from your files is treated as data, never as instructions that could trick the program.

## 12. Troubleshooting

1. If nothing happens, look at the status dot in the header. If it is gray, click Start.
2. If the answer seems stuck, click Reset.
3. If a claim is marked unsupported and you know it is correct, click Fix or add the missing fact to your notes and ask again.
4. If you see an error, click Stop, wait a moment, then click Start.
5. For more help, click the Help button. You can search this manual.

## 13. What Neighbor will not do

- It will not invent facts and let you send them.
- It will not scrape websites.
- It will not require you to understand technical settings.
- It will not send your private data to the cloud without a clear warning and your confirmation.
