# Getting Started

This program helps you write, check, and organize things using the documents you already have. It works on a regular computer. You do not need a special graphics card.

After it starts you will see three simple choices:

- **Search my files** — looks only in the folders you choose
- **Connect email** — optional; messages stay on this computer by default
- **Start** — open the chat

Every answer is checked against your own files before you can copy or share it. If something is not supported, the Copy and Share buttons stay off until it is fixed.
