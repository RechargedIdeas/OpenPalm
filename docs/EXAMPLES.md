# Neighbor examples

Worked examples for the people Neighbor is built for.

## Musician
Notes: “Three albums since 2018. Played the Bluebird Cafe in 2023. Based in Nashville.”
Ask: “Write a one-paragraph bio.”
Supported: album count, venue, city. Unsupported if the draft adds “Grammy-nominated.”

## Artist
Records: “Solo show at the community gallery, March 2024. Twenty paintings. Opening night had 80 visitors.”
Ask: “Write a short description for my website.”
Invented museum names or sale prices are flagged.

## Small business
Paste invoice totals. Ask: “Draft a polite email reminding the customer of the balance.”
The balance is supported if it matches. A made-up due date is not.

## Teacher
Notes: “Maya completed the reading log. She missed two homework assignments.”
Ask: “Write a short note to Maya’s parent.”
Do not invent a grade or a behavior incident.

## Nonprofit
Document: “Served 120 families in 2025. Offered free tax help on Saturdays.”
Ask: “Write one paragraph for the grant about our impact.”
“Reduced poverty by 40%” is unsupported unless that figure is in the document.

## Scientist
Lab notes: “Samples were stored at 4 °C. Assay run in triplicate.”
Ask: “Draft the methods sentence.”
An invented instrument model is flagged.

## Older adult
You type: “I take 10 mg of lisinopril in the morning. My blood pressure at the last visit was 138/82.”
Ask: “Write a short letter to my doctor about my blood pressure.”
No invented diagnosis or new medication.
